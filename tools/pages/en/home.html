Every bouquet is a message not yet put into words

Quỳnh Như Florist is a small wax flower shop in Thủ Dầu Một – Bình Dương (now part of Ho Chi Minh City), making wax flowers for occasions and decorative table flower stands.

Wax flowers keep their shape and colour for a long time, so the gift can stay with the recipient long after the day it is given. The shop is new. We make your flowers close to the design you chose; if a colour or flower type has to change, we tell you first. We give careful advice from your first message until the flowers are in your hands.
