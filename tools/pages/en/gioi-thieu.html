About Quỳnh Như Florist

Quỳnh Như Florist is a wax flower shop in the Thủ Dầu Một – Bình Dương area (now part of Ho Chi Minh City), making wax flowers for occasions and decorative table flower stands. Wax flowers keep their shape and colour for a long time, so each gift can stay with the recipient long after the day it is given.

The shop is new, and we make every bouquet hoping to give you a complete experience — from the first advice to the moment the flowers reach the recipient.

In less than a year, we have sent nearly 500 bouquets to customers in the area.

What is this website? This is the official website of Quỳnh Như Florist, where you can see our designs, learn how to order and contact the shop directly.

What can you find here? Wax flowers for occasions, decorative table flower stands, ordering times, answers to common questions, and the channels to talk with the shop directly.
